Baiyu Bot v1.2.0 for Android (arm64, Android 7+). Installs over v1.1.0.

- Agents are now in 3D: the same colours, eyes and accessories as before, as soft rounded figures that breathe,
  blink and hop while they work. Their motion runs off the JavaScript thread, so lists of agents stay smooth.
- A new app icon, launch screen and mark: the white agent. Four more icon colours to pick in the app.
- The launch screen says Baiyu Bot.

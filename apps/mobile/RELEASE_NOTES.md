Baiyu Bot v1.2.2 for Android (arm64, Android 7+). Installs over v1.2.1.

- Lists with rows you can swipe (the home, Activity, archived agents) scroll with the finger again. A row now opens
  only when the finger goes clearly sideways, and gives the touch back to the list as soon as it moves up or down;
  before, a scroll that drifted a little sideways was taken by the row, so the list moved a little and stuck, or a row
  opened instead.
- A tap on an open row closes it.

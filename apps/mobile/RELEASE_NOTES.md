Baiyu Bot v1.2.3 for Android (arm64, Android 7+). Installs over v1.2.2.

- PDFs open inside the app. Android's own web view can't show a PDF, so until now a PDF only showed its card and
  "send it to another app". The app now draws it with pdf.js: pages appear as you scroll, pinch to zoom, Chinese,
  Japanese and Korean text included. Nothing leaves the phone to show it; the file is downloaded once and kept in
  the app's cache.
- PDFs over 40 MB, or files pdf.js can't read, still show the card; Share opens them in another app.

中文：PDF 现在可以直接在应用里预览（逐页加载、双指缩放、支持中日韩文字）。超过 40 MB 或无法解析的文件仍显示文件卡片，可用“分享”在其他应用打开。

Baiyu Bot v1.1.0 for Android (arm64, Android 7+). Installs over v1.0.0.

- Storage settings: the bucket's form no longer closes while you type.
- Smoother lists of agents on Android (agents are redrawn less often).
- A conversation you already have opens at the tap, without waiting for the server.
- "See all" lists: hold a row to pin it to the top (and so onto the home).
- A skill's page turns it on or off for each agent.
- "Couldn't send the file" says why under it.

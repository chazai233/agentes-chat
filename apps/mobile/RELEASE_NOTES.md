Baiyu Bot v1.2.1 for Android (arm64, Android 7+). Installs over v1.2.0.

Smoother on Android:

- Tabs you aren't on stop updating until you come back, and a tab you haven't opened isn't drawn until you do. Before,
  every message or status from the server re-rendered all five tabs.
- A screen covered by another (the home under a conversation) stops updating until it shows again.
- Skies are drawn once instead of every frame, and agents in lists hold still; agents shown large still move.
- The small working marks (orbits) step 8 times a second instead of 25.
- Agent pictures are decoded at the size they are shown.
